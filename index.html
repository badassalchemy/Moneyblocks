06

Integration: Becoming the Woman Who Holds Wealth

Clearing money blocks is powerful. Rewriting your relationship with money is necessary. But abundance doesn't stabilize until your identity catches up.

Wealth Is Not a Moment. It's a Capacity.

Many women can manifest money once. A bonus. A surge. A good month. But then:

  • they feel pressure
  • they rush decisions
  • they overspend or undercharge
  • they shrink or self-sabotage

Not because they're irresponsible — but because their nervous system hasn't stabilized at that level yet.

Wealth requires capacity, not just desire.
  • make calm decisions
  • delay reaction
  • trust yourself
  • allow money to stay
Money doesn't stay with the woman who wants it. It stays with the woman who feels safe, steady, and self-led while holding it.
Guided Embodiment Practice
Stabilizing Your Next Level
Take a breath.
Place one hand on your chest.
Ask yourself:
How does my body feel when I imagine holding significantly more money?

Where do I tense?
What part of me feels alert or cautious?
Now repeat slowly, out loud if you can:
I am safe holding more.
I trust myself at higher levels.
I can lead myself through expansion.
Wealth does not destabilize me.
It supports me.
